## 1.1.0
- **NEW:** `PreventAllWeatherDamage` config (default true) blocks ALL rain/weather/seasons wear on wood everywhere, not just at the waterline.
- **NEW:** `ProtectAtWaterline` config (default true) lets you control the original waterline-only protection independently.
- Set both to false to disable the mod entirely.

## 1.0.1
- Docks and other waterline wood: also treat pieces within 2.5 m of the local water surface as not wet, so rain wear no longer eats dock planks that vanilla only counted as "dry but raining".

## 1.0.0
- Wood, hardwood, and timberwood pieces no longer wear from standing in water.
- Rain on an unroofed piece still wears it. Ship hulls are unchanged.
- Install on clients and the dedicated server.
